# Onset: what to check by hand, deploy-20261125-1633

Live: https://onset.joaotorr.es. Shipped: 68, 74, 76, 77, 78, 79, 80, 82, 84, 85, 86, 87.
Only what the specs can't prove. Nothing to check for 80, 82, 84, 85, 86 (specs or docs only, nothing to see on the live app).

Setup: a laptop with the big screen open, 2 phones (one iPhone with Safari if you have it), a second laptop browser tab. Check light and dark on each.

## 1. Bots from the big screen (68, 79)

1. On the laptop open https://onset.joaotorr.es and start a room. The big screen shows the waiting room.
2. Look at the top of the right rail: an "ADD A BOT" tile with Easy, Medium, Hard buttons. Check it in light and dark.
3. Tap Easy, then Medium, then Hard (and Easy again).
4. Join the room from a phone with the code or QR, and open the join link on the phone.
5. On the phone, look at the waiting list. No add-bot control should be there.
6. Keep adding bots until the room has 8 players.
7. Remove nothing; just look at the tile at 8 players.
8. With only bots in the room (use a fresh room, no people joined), look at Start game.
9. Join one phone, vote ready on it, and watch the game start.
10. Play a few moves and finish a round: ready, Start game, Play again should all answer normally, with no "too many requests" slowdown.
Expect: bots named "Easy bot", "Easy bot 2", "Medium bot", "Hard bot", each with a small bot mark after its name and a ✓ at once, on the big screen and in the phone's waiting list. At 8 players the three buttons are grayed out with "Room full". With bots only, Start game is disabled and reads "Need a person to play". One person plus bots starts once that person votes ready. The tile is gone once play starts. Bots do not play. Play again keeps the bots, scores reset.

## 2. Join race for the last color (74)

1. Fill a room to 7 players.
2. On two phones open the join page, pick the same last free color, and tap Join on both at the same moment.
Expect: the winner joins. The other phone comes back with "This room is full" and Join disabled. With 2 or more free colors before, it comes back with the color error and the next free color selected, and the taken color faded with the taker's initial. Hard to hit by hand; skip if it won't line up.

## 3. Locked-out entry on the phone scoreboard (76)

1. In a game with 2 phones, make both players lock out (wrong SET! claims).
2. On each phone, look at the scoreboard: the other player's locked entry and your own, in portrait, in landscape, then on a laptop at the controller page. Do it in light and in dark.
Expect: locked names and scores are gray but easy to read, not faded. The dot is faded. The red Locked tag is unchanged.

## 4. Theme and card color picks (77, 78, 87)

1. On the laptop, open https://onset.joaotorr.es in one tab. Open the settings menu and leave it open.
2. In a second tab on the same site, pick Dark (or Light, whichever differs). Pick Color-blind too.
3. In the first tab go to another site (type any address), then press Back.
4. Open the settings menu.
5. In the second tab pick Match device, then repeat steps 3 and 4.
6. Repeat 1 to 5 on https://onset.joaotorr.es/404.html (or any page that shows the error page style) for the theme only.
7. On an iPhone in Safari: pick Dark and Color-blind, then check again after more than 7 days (or note the date and check next week).
Expect: after Back, the page shows the theme and card colors you picked meanwhile, the browser bar color matches, the menu is closed and its marked choice is the new one. Match device follows the device again. On the error page, the same for the theme. On the iPhone the picks are still there after a week.
