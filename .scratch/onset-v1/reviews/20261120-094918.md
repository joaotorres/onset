## Pending decisions

- **A script can get past the in-game tap limits by joining again and again.** The limits ticket 60 built count each player's taps on their own (180 claims and 30 each of ready, No Set and End game a minute), so one fast player never gets a whole table on shared Wi-Fi refused. But one address may join 20 times a minute, every join gets a full allowance of its own, and a joined player stays until the room has been idle for 24 hours. A script that keeps joining its own rooms can therefore still send far more than the limits intend and slow every room, which is the load SPEC §5 Rate limits gives as their reason. People playing never meet either limit.
  1. Add a loose ceiling per address on top (recommended): about 2,500 in-game actions a minute from one address, above the 2,160 a full table of 8 could send at the limits and far above real play (about 5 actions a claim), so a script is capped whatever it joins. No table notices. Changing it later is one number.
  2. Leave it: the limits stop one runaway client or a simple script, and a determined one can still slow the rooms. Nothing to build, and the ceiling can be added whenever it is wanted.
