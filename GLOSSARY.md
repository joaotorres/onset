# Onset

Real-time multiplayer Set. Each player plays on their own device; a shared big screen is optional.

## Language

### Screens

**Player screen**:
One player's own view of a game, on a phone or a computer, from which they call SET!, pick cards and vote.
_Avoid_: Controller, phone, remote

**Big screen**:
An optional shared display of a game, such as a TV or laptop the whole table can see. It is not a player.
_Avoid_: Board, TV, host screen

### Game

**Room**:
One game, identified by its six-character room code, from waiting through play to the end.
_Avoid_: Lobby, session, match

**Board**:
The face-up cards in play, 12 to 18 of them.
_Avoid_: Table, grid

**Set**:
Three cards whose four attributes are each all the same or all different.

**Claim**:
A player's call of SET!, which gives them the sole right to pick three cards before the claim window closes.
_Avoid_: Call, turn

**Lockout**:
The 15 seconds after a wrong or timed-out claim during which that player cannot claim again.
_Avoid_: Penalty, cooldown

**No Set call**:
A countdown any player starts when they believe the board holds no Set; when every active player agrees, or it runs out, three more cards are dealt.
_Avoid_: Pass, skip

**Refusal**:
A player's answer to a No Set call that there is a Set to find ("There's a Set"): it cancels the call and blocks No Set for everyone for 15 seconds. One per board, no penalty.
_Avoid_: Veto, reject, objection

**Ready vote**:
The vote every player casts to start a room, or to play again once it has ended.

**Standings**:
Every player in a room ranked by score, ties sharing a rank.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard, podium (the podium is the big screen's top three)
